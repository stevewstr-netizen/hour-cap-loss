Old pay vs new pay

Drop a Workday paycheck or time-entry PDF. It stays on this computer. The left side is the old method. The right side is the new method. Pay is still calculated with the regular-rate overtime formula. PTO counts toward pension and 401(k) in both. Holiday is paid at the regular rate and counts in neither. Off-shift counts at straight time. The 1.5 does not. Overtime counts only on the old side. Employer 401(k) is half of what you put in, up to 4%. If you put in nothing, the employer puts in nothing.
