Weekly old vs new

The paycheck does not change. Overtime is still paid. Hours over 40 in a week do not count toward the 401(k) or the pension. Company 401(k) is half of what you put in, and it stops at 4% of counted pay. Put in 8% to get the full 4%. Put in nothing and the company puts in nothing. Your own deferral also stops at the IRS annual limit.
