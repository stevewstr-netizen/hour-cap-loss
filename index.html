Weekly old vs new

Gross uses regular hours at straight time, off-shift hours at time-and-a-half, and overtime at the stub rate. The paycheck is the same old and new. Only hours counted for the 401(k) and pension change, and those hours are capped at 40 a week. A smaller pre-tax 401(k) deferral raises taxable income. The tax line is that extra income times your federal bracket plus Colorado's 4.4% flat rate. It does not change Social Security or Medicare.
