401(k) and pension use straight-time pay only. Off-shift hours count. The 1.5 paid on those hours does not. Overtime hours are paid and do not count. Hours you do not work do not count and do not create overtime. Company 401(k) is half of what you put in, up to 4% of straight-time counted pay. Enter either your percent or the dollar amount on the check. The federal bracket comes from the tax table.
