Weekly old vs new

Gross uses regular hours at straight time, off-shift hours at time-and-a-half, and overtime at the stub rate. The paycheck is the same old and new. Only hours counted for the 401(k) and pension change, and those hours are capped at 40 a week. Company 401(k) is half of what you put in, up to 4% of counted straight-time pay. The year-to-date box is your 401(k) line, not the company 401k ER line.
